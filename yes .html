Jollof Rice and Turkey

Description

Jollof rice and turkey is a legendary West African culinary pairing that brings unmatched vibrant color, bold spices, and rich flavors to the plate.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of sugar
  • 2 cups of flour
  • 1/2 cup of butter
  • 1 teaspoon of vanilla extract
  • 1/2 teaspoon of baking powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon of salt

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. In a large bowl, cream together the sugar and butter until light and fluffy.
  3. Add the vanilla extract and mix well.
  4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt.
  5. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients, mixing until just combined.
  6. Pour the batter into a greased baking pan and spread it evenly.
  7. Bake for 25-30 minutes or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  8. Allow to cool before serving. Enjoy!
